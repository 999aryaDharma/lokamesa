## Status definitions

- `FUTURE` — idea captured but not specified enough to implement.
- `CANDIDATE` — shaped feature direction, pending owner approval.
- `SPECIFIED` — implementation-facing specification and sprint decomposition exist, but not in active execution.
- `PROMOTED` — explicitly moved into the active sprint board.

## Current rule

The six `1.1.0`–`1.6.0` feature lines are `SPECIFIED` and remain outside the active V1 sprint count. `1.7.0` is `SPECIFIED` (`WB-01…WB-05`, ADR-003 accepted) and `1.10.0` is `SPECIFIED` (`DB-01…DB-07`, native direction locked by ADR-004); both stay outside the active V1 board until promoted. `1.8.0` and `1.9.0` remain `CANDIDATE`.
